## Levels

Triggering kernel: exponential in time (β = 0.3), Gaussian in space (σ = 0.03) with a centre that drifts at speed v; entanglement ρ = vβ/σ. Background and branching ratio (realized ≈ 0.70) are held fixed across levels.

| Level | v | ρ | Config |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 | 0 | 0 | `hawkesnest_entanglement_L0` |
| L1 | 0.1 | 1 | `hawkesnest_entanglement_L1` |
| L2 | 0.2 | 2 | `hawkesnest_entanglement_L2` |
| L3 | 0.3 | 3 | `hawkesnest_entanglement_L3` |

Excess test NLL over the true process (nats/event, mean over three seeds; paper Table 3):

| Model | L0 | L1 | L2 | L3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Separable Hawkes fit (reference) | 0.00 | 0.08 | 0.17 | 0.25 |
| AutoSTPP | 0.04 | 0.06 | 0.07 | 0.10 |
| DeepSTPP | 0.21 | 0.35 | 0.50 | 0.63 |
| NSMPP | 1.45 | 1.46 | 1.50 | 1.56 |
| NJSDE | 0.96 | 0.97 | 0.93 | 0.95 |
| RMTPP | 2.25 | 2.29 | 2.32 | 2.40 |
| THP | 2.14 | 2.18 | 2.21 | 2.29 |
| DSTPP (variational bound) | 3.29 | 3.32 | 3.33 | 3.35 |

AutoSTPP is the only model below the separable reference at L1–L3. SMASH has no likelihood. The generator code, the true-process parameters and SHA-256 checksums are in the folder `hawkesnest_entanglement/` of the [repository](https://huggingface.co/datasets/seahorse-stpp/seahorse_synthetic_stpp).
